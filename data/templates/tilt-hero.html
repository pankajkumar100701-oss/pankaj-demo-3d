FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my money safe with Orbit?

Deposits are held with licensed partner banks and protected up to the legal limit. Every card has instant freeze, biometric approval and real-time alerts.

Are there really no fees?

Standard is free forever. Exchange is fee-free up to $1,000 a month on Standard and unlimited on Plus. We'll always show the exact rate before you confirm.

How long does the metal card take?

Your virtual card works the moment you're approved. Physical cards arrive in 3–5 working days in most countries.

Can I switch or cancel my plan?

Yes — upgrade, downgrade or cancel from Settings at any time. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata.
