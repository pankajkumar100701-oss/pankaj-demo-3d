The studio

Three people, one north-facing room in Grünerløkka.

Halden was founded in 2016 by photographer Ingrid Halden and art director Tobias Ree. We work in small crews, shoot mostly on medium format, and plan every day around where the sun will be — not where the client’s calendar says it should be.

Every commission ends with a box of hand-made prints. Files get lost; paper tends to stay on a wall.
