FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Our team replies within an hour on weekdays.

What counts as a run?

A run is one complete execution of a workflow, no matter how many steps it has. Failed runs that never reach a second step are free.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated to the day; downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

What happens if I go over my run limit?

Nothing breaks. Workflows keep running and we’ll email you at 80% and 100%. Extra runs are billed at $4 per 1,000 — or upgrade to make it go away.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits and startups?

Yes — registered nonprofits get 50% off any plan, and startups under two years old get Pro free for their first six months.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia), your choice. Everything is encrypted at rest and in transit, and we never train models on your data.
