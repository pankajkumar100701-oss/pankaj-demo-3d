Programs

Four ways to fall in love with the night sky.

Whether you've never touched a telescope or you're chasing faint galaxies, there's a seat for you under our dome.

Public star parties

Every new-moon Saturday at Hollow Ridge, with eight club telescopes and guides at every eyepiece.

Telescope library

Borrow a Dobsonian, refractor or star tracker for two weeks — setup lesson and red flashlight included.

Astrophotography

Monthly workshops from first Moon shot to stacking deep-sky images, taught by members who've been published.

Junior astronomers

Hands-on sessions for ages 8–14: build a sundial, map constellations and earn the Meridian Explorer badge.
