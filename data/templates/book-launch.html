About the author

Mira Sen writes about the hours most people sleep through.

Born in Pune and raised between Bristol and the Kerala coast, Mira spent eight years as a night archivist before her debut, Salt & Lantern, was shortlisted for the Ember Prize. She lives by a very loud river with two cats and too many clocks.
