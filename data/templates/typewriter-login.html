What it does

Fewer features.
On purpose.

Three tools, sharpened until they disappear. Everything else stays outside the margin.

01

Focus mode

Dims every line but the one you are on. Notifications wait outside; the typewriter clack is optional.

02

Version history

Every sitting is saved as a fresh carbon copy. Scroll back through drafts and lift a lost sentence in one click.

03

Export

Send a manuscript to .docx, PDF, ePub or plain Markdown, typeset with proper margins and curly quotes.

Write without noise

The page, and nothing else.

No feeds. No badges. No little red numbers. Just a cursor, a blank page and the sound of your own sentences arriving one at a time. MARGIN closes the door so the work can open.
